# Privacy

Primer is an instructions-only skill. It has no server, account system,
analytics, telemetry, advertising, or persistent data store, and its publisher
does not receive user prompts, files, search queries, or generated outputs.

Primer runs inside the AI product where a user installs or invokes it. That
product may process prompts, files, links, web searches, and outputs under its
own privacy terms and account settings. Primer instructs the host not to place
confidential or privileged facts into public web searches.

Questions about this policy can be raised through the repository's
[issue tracker](https://github.com/nicktongwj/primer-skill/issues).
